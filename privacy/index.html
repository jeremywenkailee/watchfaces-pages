Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all Wear OS watch face apps published by Jeremy Lee, including Trek Console (com.jwklee.trekconsole) and Simplicity (com.jwklee.simplicity), collectively "the apps."

Information collection and use

The apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. They do not request any Android permissions, and they contain no analytics, advertising, or network code.

Each app displays "complications" — small data tiles such as battery level, step count, weather, or notifications — using Android's built-in Wear OS complication system. This data is supplied directly by the complication provider apps you have already installed and granted access to on your watch (for example, the system Battery, Steps, or Google Weather providers). The watch face itself never accesses this data outside of what the Wear OS system renders on screen, and never stores or transmits it anywhere.

Third-party services

Because complications are provided by other apps on your device, their data practices are governed by those apps' own privacy policies, not this one. Relevant examples include:

  • Google Play Services

Children's privacy

The apps do not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
